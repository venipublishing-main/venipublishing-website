Delete Veni Account

Eligible accounts can permanently delete their sign-in, profile and library entitlements.

  • If you own a Reserve business, arrange an ownership transfer first. Accounts linked to Reserve records also require a retention review before deletion.
  • This cannot be undone.
  • It does not delete Veni Publishing’s public content.
  • Future legally required transaction records, if introduced, will need separate retention rules before commerce launches.
