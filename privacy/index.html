Privacy

Privacy policy

A privacy-conscious website with an optional adult-directed Veni Account layer.

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

Public website

The public Veni Publishing website does not use first-party behavioural advertising or require an account for ordinary browsing. Free Veni Kids resources remain accessible without unnecessary personal information.

Veni Account

Adults who choose to create a Veni Account provide an email address and may provide a display name. Authentication and the current account database are operated through Supabase. Authentication sessions are stored in the browser so users can remain signed in.

Library and access records

Veni Account may store private entitlement records describing digital works or platform access attached to the signed-in user. Users can read only their own entitlement records through the website.

Account deletion

Current beta users can request permanent account deletion from Veni Account Settings. Deleting the authentication user also removes the current profile and entitlement records linked to that user through the account data model. If financial transactions or legally required records are introduced later, this notice and the deletion rules must be updated before launch.

Email and password operations

Account confirmation, password recovery and email-change messages are sent through the authentication service. Veni Publishing does not display or store users' readable passwords.

Children's privacy

Veni Kids remains a child-facing, primarily anonymous experience. It does not ask children to create Veni Accounts or submit names, ages, schools, photographs or contact details. Account, purchase and contact functions reached from Veni Kids remain parent- or educator-directed.

Cookies and browser storage

No first-party behavioural advertising or analytics cookies are included in the current site. Authentication uses browser storage and session mechanisms necessary to keep a signed-in session working.

External services

The site may link to external services such as Instagram, Amazon, Payhip or other providers. Those services operate under their own privacy terms. Supabase is currently used for Veni Account authentication and application data.

Contact

Privacy-related enquiries can be sent to veni.publishing@gmail.com.
