## 3. Account decisions and product boundaries

The private Account schema records verified organization IDs, unique Reserve business-to-organization bindings and time-bounded publish decisions. A composite foreign key enforces the exact business and organization. Decision UUIDs identify operator records. Each business has at most one open grant, based on a subscription or a free pilot. Account operators close the previous decision and record a successor in one transaction; explicit revocation takes effect on the next entitlement check. Reserve's private invoker interface evaluates only the specific business at database time under the onboarding executor's owner policy. Application roles have no direct read or write on the grant ledger. The candidate seeds zero customer grants. Billing integration, operator identity evidence and approval of a particular business remain operational decisions.

Reserve still requires a live, correctly classified confidential client, a matching user and session, current consent and business membership. Existing draft reads and saves continue during Account outages. Activation and any future publication must consult the current decision; a stored ACTIVE tenant status is insufficient. Performance requires its own registered client, live session and consent, product entitlement and organization membership. Its database role is `performance_api`. Website and Arcana direct sessions retain access to their own private rows. Restrictive policies and a direct-session check on the account-deletion handler close delegated access to those surfaces. The handler's live hosted version remains v2 pending separate review.
